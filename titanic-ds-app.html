Key data science findings

Why sex dominates

The evacuation policy "women and children first" is directly encoded in the data, making sex the strongest feature at 28.6% importance.

Class & deck location

Higher class cabins were physically closer to lifeboats on upper decks — spatial privilege directly translated into survival odds.

Title as proxy feature

Title (Mr, Mrs, Miss, Master) encodes sex, age, and social rank simultaneously — the 2nd most important feature at 16.2%.
